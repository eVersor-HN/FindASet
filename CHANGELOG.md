# What's New in FindASet

Built forward. Tracked clearly. Newest first.

---

## 1.18 — 2026-08-17

- Macros can start over: clear just the day you are viewing, or wipe the entire logged history — each behind its own confirmation step.
- Goals and favourites are never touched by either reset.

## 1.17 — 2026-08-14

- The voice stopped talking over itself: counted numbers are spoken as words, played slower and through a clearer tone, and the rep count now waits until the announcement has actually finished.
- Announcements got shorter — the rep target is already on screen and no longer read out at the top and tail of every set.
- The voice releases its memory after ten unused minutes outside a workout, freeing the device for everything else.
- Completing a set now means "carry on": a paused set completes correctly, timed sets can be completed instead of only skipped, and timed call-outs read out the time that is left.
- The live notification counts every second instead of jumping in steps of five.
- New in-workout overview: everything done with its result, what is running and what is still coming — with corrections you can make on the spot, typed in or nudged with steppers.
- The rest screen carries the next exercise's target and what you managed on it last time; a tempo bar above the ring opens and closes at the exercise's own rhythm.
- A finished workout can write itself up as a shareable report; the newest twenty are kept and managed from a new settings card.
- Training plan: every strength day opens with easy skipping, the running week is structured into base, long, recovery and interval sessions, and rows no longer need a table.
- Runs stopped being logged as lifts — a pace note like "6 km/h" is no longer read as six kilograms, and a number only counts as weight when it says kg.

## 1.16 — 2026-08-11

- Food history lists each product exactly once — the way you logged it last — instead of one row per portion size.
- Removing a product clears it at every portion, rather than leaving its other portions behind.
- The filler pool's history list follows the same rule.

## 1.15 — 2026-08-11

- Food history now leads with what you actually ate last, ordered by real time of use rather than by the day an entry was filed under.
- Saved foods take their place in the same time-ordered list instead of sitting pinned above everything else.

## 1.14 — 2026-08-10

- The easy training days became a real outdoor run instead of a machine session, and Sunday joined them — leaving one true rest day in the week.

## 1.13 — 2026-07-27

- Stand today's session down with one tap: macro targets, reminder and readouts switch to rest for that day without touching the plan. Tap again to put it back.
- The spoken screen-entry line is limited to once a day and no longer clears its throat first.
- Audit round: the voice ducks your music while it speaks, timers survive a dark screen, and deletions ask before they act.
- Long histories load and save faster, and logging a set no longer rewrites years of history.

## 1.12 — 2026-07-25

- Building a workout became five simple steps, from name to review, with a running duration estimate.
- The label scanner goes multilingual and tells calories from kilojoules reliably.
- Optional, off-by-default barcode lookup: fetch exact nutrition values online when a label is unreadable.
- The Analysis voice reads your actual log instead of speaking in generalities, and the operator name is spoken as a word rather than letter by letter.
- Settings moved into a terminal-style self-test line on the boot screen.

## 1.11 — 2026-07-22

- Boot report type is 5% smaller, so the startup sequence sits more comfortably on screen.

## 1.10 — 2026-07-20

- Every tap stays fast: saving moved off the interface thread and the log is indexed by day.
- A workout survives the app being killed — it resumes where it stood instead of restarting.
- The boot report replays on every app open and fits the screen without scrolling.
- Faster startup, a lower memory ceiling while scanning, a notification bar that takes your accent colour, and honest touch targets throughout.

## 1.9 — 2026-07-20

## 1.0 — 2026-06-30

- First public release.
- Guided workout player: timeline, circular timer, voice cues, spoken rest countdowns and session summary.
- Rep and set logging with weight per set, estimated 1RM tracking and announced personal records.
- Training plans, calendar and logbook with heatmap, streaks and per-day notes.
- Macro tracking with a food library, favourites, fuel-tank goals, a live daily notification and the FILL MACROS calculator.
- Multilingual food database, searchable in 15 languages.
- On-device nutrition-label scanner with an energy cross-check.
- Body-weight log with goal and trend.
- Neural offline announcer with selectable voices, styles and effects.
- Local backup export and import. No account, no cloud, no ads, no tracking.
